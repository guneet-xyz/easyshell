`cut -d,` and `awk -F,` split at **every** comma, including the ones inside quotes. Look at the quotes instead: if you split a line at `"`, the pieces at even positions (2nd, 4th, ...) are exactly the insides of the quoted fields.

```bash
echo 'a,"b, c",d' | awk -F'"' '{print $2}'
```

```
b, c
```
