Once the inner commas are out of the way, take the second field and count the lines that match it **exactly**. `grep -x` only matches whole lines, and `grep -c` counts them:

```bash
awk -F'"' -v OFS='"' '{for (i = 2; i <= NF; i += 2) gsub(/,/, ";", $i)} 1' venues.csv | cut -d, -f2 | grep -cx '"Portland; OR"'
```
