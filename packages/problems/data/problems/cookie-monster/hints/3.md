Two requests: one to log in and fill the jar, and one to visit the dashboard with it.

```sh
curl -s -c jar.txt --data-urlencode "username=$(sed -n 1p login.txt)" --data-urlencode "password=$(sed -n 2p login.txt)" http://localhost:4000/login > /dev/null
curl -s -b jar.txt http://localhost:4000/dashboard | jq -r .welcome
```
