# Problem Statement

You've decided to start journaling. Therapists recommend it, influencers swear by it, and your houseplant has stopped listening to you. There's just one condition: you refuse to leave the terminal. No notebook, no app, no scented gel pens. Just you, a blinking cursor and your feelings.

There's a catch. Somewhere in your home folder there might be an old `diary.txt` from your teenage years, full of poetry about Kevin. (Sample couplet: _"Kevin, Kevin, you're my sun / you texted back, now I'm undone."_) Nobody can ever read that. Today's entry goes in, and the poetry goes away. Forever.

# Instructions

1. Write exactly this line, followed by a newline, into a file named `diary.txt` in the current directory:

   ```
   Dear Diary, today I learned redirection.
   ```

2. If `diary.txt` already exists, replace its contents entirely. Not a single word of the old diary may survive.
3. Leave every other file untouched.
4. Don't print anything to the terminal. Diaries are private.
