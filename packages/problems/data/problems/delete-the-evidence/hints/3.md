The `DELETE` responses would end up in your output too, so send them to `/dev/null`. Then ask Legal for the certificate:

```sh
curl -s http://localhost:4000/posts | jq '.[] | select(.author == "Melon Usk") | .id' |
  xargs -I{} curl -s -X DELETE http://localhost:4000/posts/{} > /dev/null
curl -s http://localhost:4000/legal/clearance | jq -r .certificate
```
