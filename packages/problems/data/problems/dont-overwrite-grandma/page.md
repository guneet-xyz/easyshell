# Problem Statement

Grandma has finally trusted you with `recipes.txt`: her life's work, typed with one finger over three years, with caps lock on the entire time. There is exactly one copy. Grandma doesn't believe in backups. "Backups are for people who make mistakes, dear."

You want to leave your mark on the family legacy by adding one secret ingredient to the end of the file. Just remember what `>` did to your teenage poetry in Dear Diary. One wrong character and three generations of lasagna are gone, and you're spending every Christmas from now on alone. No pressure.

# Instructions

1. Add this line to the **end** of `recipes.txt`:

   ```
   - 1 pinch of shell magic
   ```

2. Your line must end with a newline. `recipes.txt` always ends with a newline already, so your line goes on a line of its own.
3. Everything that was already in `recipes.txt` must stay exactly as it is, in the same order.
4. Leave every other file untouched.
