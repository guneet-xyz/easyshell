`awk` can remember things between lines. You can keep track of the section you're in, and only look at keys while you're inside the right one:

```bash
awk '/^\[/ {section = $0} section == "[fruits]" {print}' file.ini
```

With `-F' *= *'`, `awk` splits each line at the `=` and swallows the spaces around it, so `$1` is the key and `$2` is the value.
