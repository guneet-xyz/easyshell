# Problem Statement

After seven and a half million years of computation, the supercomputer Deep Thought has finally finished. The pan-dimensional mice who built it have gathered around the printer, holding their breath.

Deep Thought, being a computer, didn't just print the answer. It printed its entire work log into `guide.txt`: every coffee break, every recount, every existential crisis. The answer is in there somewhere. On line 42. Obviously.

The mice don't have another seven and a half million years to scroll. Help them out.

# Instructions

1. Print **only** line 42 of `guide.txt` (the first line of the file is line 1).
2. Print it exactly as it is, including any spaces at the start or in the middle of the line.

> **Note:** Deep Thought loves to pad its log with lines that _sound_ like the answer. Trust the line number, not the vibes. The file always has at least 42 lines.
