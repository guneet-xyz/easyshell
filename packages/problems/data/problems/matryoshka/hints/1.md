Your toolbox:

- `tar -xf archive.tar` extracts a tar archive. Add `-z` for `.tar.gz`, `-j` for `.tar.bz2` and `-J` for `.tar.xz`.
- `unzip -q archive.zip` extracts a zip without chatting about it.
- `gunzip file.gz`, `bunzip2 file.bz2` and `unxz file.xz` decompress a file and remove the compressed one.
