First, get just the hour out of every line. The timestamp is the 4th field, `[14/Nov/2026:21:05:33`, and the hour starts at its 14th character: `awk '{print substr($4, 14, 2)}'`. Or cut everything up to `[` away and take the second `:`-separated field of what's left. Either way, don't let the IPv6 addresses fool you.
