You don't have to encode anything by hand. `curl` can do it for you: `--data-urlencode "q=some text"` encodes the value, and `-G` (`--get`) puts the data into the URL's query string of a `GET` request instead of a `POST` body.

`jq` can do it too: its `@uri` filter percent-encodes a string, and `jq -rR` reads raw lines from a file.
