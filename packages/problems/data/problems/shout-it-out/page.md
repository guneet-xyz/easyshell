# Problem Statement

You've just finished the launch announcement for the latest feature of your Twitter clone. It's clear, it's friendly, and it has exactly one emoji. You're proud of it.

Marketing has reviewed it and has one note: "It doesn't feel EXCITING enough." Their solution, as always, is caps lock. Unfortunately the caps lock key on the marketing laptop broke last quarter from overuse, so the job lands on your desk.

You could retype the whole thing with your pinky glued to Shift. Or you could let the shell do the shouting while you sip your coffee in dignified silence.

# Instructions

1. Print the contents of `announcement.txt` with every lowercase letter (`a` to `z`) converted to uppercase.
2. Everything else stays exactly as it is: letters that are already uppercase, digits, punctuation, spaces, blank lines and emoji.

# Example

If `announcement.txt` contains:

```
Big news: we're on version 2.0! 🎉
```

you should print:

```
BIG NEWS: WE'RE ON VERSION 2.0! 🎉
```
