GRANDMA'S SECRET CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIES
(do not show this to your aunt)

- 2 1/4 cups flour
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups chocolate chips
- the secret ingredient: a pinch of salt and a lot of love

Bake at 375 F for 10 minutes. Eat 3 straight from the tray.
